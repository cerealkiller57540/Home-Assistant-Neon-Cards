Compatibility

What each card needs, what it does without WebGL, and how it behaves on phones and tablets.

Android WebViews, including the Home Assistant companion app, keep at most 8 WebGL contexts per page and drop the oldest one, which blanks a card. Every WebGL card below uses a single context; if a view carries many of them, the cards without WebGL cost nothing on that budget.
